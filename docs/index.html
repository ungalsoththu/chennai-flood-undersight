Lead: NEM 2026 Episode 1 — not publicly predicted (17–18 Oct 2026)

Chennai's heaviest spell since Michaung (Parrys ~361 mm, city average 105+ mm/24h; roads flooded across north Chennai) arrived sixteen days into a monsoon whose forecast-publication chain stopped on 30-09-2026. During the flood the portal wouldn't load from abroad, and at home it was an empty shell — no current readings on any page. Yet the engine was running inside, carrying forecasts stamped 18 October. A live forecast engine that publishes nothing during a flood is a disclosure failure, not a forecasting failure. The team's gauge totals reached the public only as @TNFloodmodeller tweets; the only formal predictions were IMD's district alerts.
