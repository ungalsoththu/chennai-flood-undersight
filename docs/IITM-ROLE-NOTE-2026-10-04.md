# IIT-Madras's role in RTFF & SDSS — the Workshop page and the continuity question — 2026-10-04

**Question:** the portal's `/Master/Workshop` page shows 2021–22-era presentations. Is there continuous IIT-M work on flood monitoring / hydrological mapping in Chennai behind the "IIT-M supervision" label?

## Is the IIT-M role continuous? Yes — but documented everywhere except the project portal

The continuity evidence, assembled from sources already in the report corpus:

- **Pre-2021 (academic groundwork):** the PSA-funded "Expert System" consortium (IIT-B/IIT-M/Anna Univ/IISc/NCCR) slide in ws_252 shows the research program predating the tender; the TOI 2026 interview with the IIT-M professor describes technical supervision since the post-2015 sanction.
- **2021:** IIT-M press release (Nov 2021): researchers braved **Cyclone Nivar** to collect real flood data — field campaigns feeding the pilot design.
- **NEM 2021 pilot → NEM 2022–23 operationalization:** Dhamodaran et al., *Current Science* 127(1), Jul 2024 — the peer-reviewed record of building and running the system, IIT-M authors included.
- **2024 (live work):** Citizen Matters — IIT-M team running a citizen-science app with **~2,000 residents logging flood water levels**, and the **4-km, 7-ensemble IIT-M model** behind the street-level forecasts.
- **2025→:** "IIT-M technical supervision" continues to be named on the live portal's AboutUs.

What is missing: the portal itself carries **no post-2021 workshop record, no publications list, no supervision-agreement disclosure** — the continuity is documented by academia (papers, press) and not by the project that benefits from it. Same pattern as the licence and O&M gaps: the relationship exists, its terms are unpublishable from the state's side.

## Don't conflate the two "IITMs"

The Revenue policy note's ₹32.48-crore IoT surge (row 31) mentions a joint site survey "by **IITM, Pune** and TNSDMA, April–June 2026" — that is the **Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology, Pune** (IMD's research arm, sensor/rain-gauge technology), **not** IIT Madras. Both institutions appear in the project's orbit; only IIT-M holds the supervision role.
