                                1.1 Development Objective
                                  Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 21-Dec-2023)
                                  To strengthen urban management and improve access to efficient and climate resilient urban water and sanitation
                                  services in participating ULBs in Tamil Nadu

                               3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS


                                3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
                                  Five Five Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) have achieved the Program Development Objective indicator target for
                                  own-source revenue increase of above 65 percent over the FY 2021–22 baseline, and all 21 ULBs have met
Public Disclosure Authorized




                                  the Minimum Conditions under Disbursement-linked Indicator (DLI) 4. The value of municipal bonds issued
                                  has exceeded the end target of US$30 million, and the 50 percent target for women in decision-making
                                  positions in City Climate Action Plan Committees has been achieved. Water and sanitation (WSS) sub-
                                  projects are being implemented in 21 participating ULBs. The Independent Verification Agency (IVA) has verified
                                  the achievement of the DLIs related to increase in own source revenues, improved fundamental institutional capacity,
                                  strengthened institutional capacity to manage resources in a sustainable, participatory, and green manner,
                                  implementation of WSS investments incorporating climate resilience guidelines, signing of Performance-based Contracts,
                                  and increased access to financial markets.
