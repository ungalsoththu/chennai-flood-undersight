                                1.1 Development Objective
                                  Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 21-Dec-2023)
                                  To strengthen urban management and improve access to efficient and climate resilient urban water and sanitation
                                  services in participating ULBs in Tamil Nadu

                                3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
                                  The World Bank loan for the operation became effective on April 22, 2024. The Independent Verification Agency has
                                  verified the achievement of the Disbursement Linked Indicator on own source revenue increase and the corresponding
                                  disbursement is being processed by the World Bank. Three Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) have met the Program
                                  Development Objective indicator "ULBs with Own Source Revenue Increase of above 65% over the respective baseline in
                                  FY 21-22". Contracts for several investment sub-projects have been signed, while procurement of the remaining contracts
                                  is at an advanced stage.
