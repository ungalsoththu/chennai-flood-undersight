1. Background

The World Bank assisted Tamil Nadu Sustainable Urban Development Project (TNSUDP)
taken up to improve urban services in the participating urban local bodies in a financially
sustainable manner and to pilot improved urban management practices in select cities
which administrative sanction was accorded vide Government order G.O.(Ms.)No.5,
Municipal Administration and Water Supply Department, dated 13.01.2016. The total outlay
of the project is US $ 600 million equivalent to about Rs.3,831 crore, of which World Bank
assistance is us $ 400 million equivalent to about Rs.2,554 crore.
The Government of Tamii Nadu have accorded in-principle approval vide Government
Order first read above to initiate the preparations for seeking Additional Financing ass
Uunder Tamil Nadu Sustainable Urban Development Project with an outlay of USD 450 m
[World Bank's additional financing of USD 300 million and counterpart financing
Government of Tamil Nadu of USD 150 million]. The name of the project has been
changed from Tamil Nadu Sustainable Urban Deveiopment Project-Additional Financi
{TNSUDP-AF) to Tamil Nadu Climate Resilient Urban Development Program (TNCRUDP).
Further the Government of Tamil Nadu opted to avail the financial assistance from Wo
Bank in Japanese Yen (JPY).

The total outlay of Tamil Nadu Climate Resilient Urban Development Program is Rs.3,
crore (equivalent to JPY 69.6797 billion), of which World Bank financing is Rs.2,49
(equivalent to JPY 45.2056 bilion) and counterpart funding is Rs.1,348.27 crore (equ
JPY 24.4741 billion) from Government of India, Government of Tamil Nadu and other sources.

2. Accounting Policies: Accounting Policies of Project Development Grant Fund (C
Component 2 - Technical Assistance)
    a) Basis of Accounting
    The financial statements have been prepared under the historical cost convention on an
    accrual basis and in accordance with applicable accounting standards issued by The
    Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.
    b) Revenue Recognition
    Interest earned on the term deposits is recognized on a time proportion basis on the
    outstanding balances at the applicable rates.

    c) Transfer of surplus
    The balance in income and Expenditure Account is transferred every year to the Grant
    from Government Account.

    d) Grant from Government Account
    The balance in Grant from Government Account represents net of grants received from
    the Government of Tamil Nadu, transfer of balance from Income and Expenditure
    Account, disbursement of technical assistance grant to consultancy assignments & other
    eligible disbursements, if any.

e) Nature of Operation
The PDGF is technical assistance grant to support consultancy assignments for creation
of urban infrastructure, provision of urban facities and services, up-gradation of
living standards of urban population and improvement in the delivery of urban services
to such population. The PDGF will be used to provide grant to carry out consultancy
assignments, to operate and manage resource mobilisation programs and to carry out
capacity building & development and training.

e) Basis of claiming reimbursement from World Bank
The grant from Project Development Grant Fund (PDGF) for the consultancy works
awarded under TNCRUDP is disbursed based on the contract documents executed and
based on the stages of the consultancy. On disbursement of the amount to the
consultant(s), claims are forwarded on a quarterly basis in the Interim Unaudited
Financial Report (IUFR) to Controller of Aid Accounts and Audit (CAAA), Department of
Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. The CAAA on approval will in
turn forward the same to the World Bank.

f) Monitoring Mechanism
The grant from PDGF, the technical assistance grant component will be used to support
the participating Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) and other entities towards consultancy
services. The objectives of the Fund are to finance the costs of technical assistance for
strengthening of the planning and implementation capacities of ULBs in Tamil Nadu and
the Project Executing Agency. The audit of PDGF is carried out by a firm of Chartered
Accountants.
