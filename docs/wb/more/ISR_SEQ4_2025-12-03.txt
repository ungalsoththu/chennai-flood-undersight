                                  Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 21-Dec-2023)

                               3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
Public Disclosure Authorized




                                3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
                                  Five Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) have met the target of the Program Development Objective indicator "ULBs with Own
                                  Source Revenue Increase of above 65% over the respective baseline in FY 21-22". Water and sanitation (WSS) sub-
                                  projects are being implemented in 21 participating ULBs. All 21 ULBs have met the Minimum Conditions for FY2024-25
                                  for Disbursement Linked Indicator (DLI) 4, while the end target of 50 percent for "Women holding decision-making
                                  positions in City Climate Action Plan Committees" has been achieved. The Independent Verification Agency has verified
                                  the achievement of the DLIs related to increase in own source revenues, improved fundamental institutional
                                  capacity, implementation of WSS investments incorporating climate resilience guidelines, signing of Performance-based
                                  Contracts, and increased access to financial markets.
