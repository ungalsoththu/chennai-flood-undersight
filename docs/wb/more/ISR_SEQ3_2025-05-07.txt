                                  Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 21-Dec-2023)

                               3. KEY ISSUES & STATUS
Public Disclosure Authorized




                                3.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
                                  Three Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) (Dindigul, Tambaram, Thiruvarur) have met the Program Development Objective
                                  indicator "ULBs with Own Source Revenue Increase of above 65% over the respective baseline in FY 21-22". 27 out of 28
                                  contracts for water and sanitation investment sub-projects have been signed and their implementation has started. In
                                  addition, 18 ULBs have met the Minimum Conditions for FY2023-24 as per Disbursement Linked Indicator (DLI) 4, while
                                  the end target (50%) of "Women holding decision-making positions in City Climate Action Plan Committees" has been
                                  achieved. The Independent Verification Agency has verified the achievement of the Disbursement Linked Indicator on
                                  own source revenue increase, improvement in institutional capacity, and the signing of Performance-based Contracts; the
                                  corresponding disbursement of US$22 million has been processed by the World Bank.
