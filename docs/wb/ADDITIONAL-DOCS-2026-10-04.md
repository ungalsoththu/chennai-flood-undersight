What they add to §5.3 of the report:

## 1. The flagship's silence is program-long, not an ISR-Seq-5 quirk
- **ISR Seq 1–4: zero mentions** of flood forecasting, RTFF, CRTFF or the control rooms, in any seq. (Seq 5, Jun 2026, was already shown silent.) Four years of Bank progress reporting, not one word about the project its own procurement plans fund.
- **The PAD (Nov 2023) cites floods 25 times** — as country/sector rationale (climate risk, CCAPs, drainage planning, DLI 3/6/7 framework for the 21 ULBs) — and **never names RTFF & SDSS**. The flagship appears nowhere in the program document, appraisal narrative, or results architecture.
- **The fiduciary paper trail is also silent**: the internal-audit TOR (2025-12-11) and both 2026-05-29 audit reports contain zero flood references. TNUIFSL's audited scope does not include the flagship.
- Program objective check: PDO = "urban management + climate-resilient **water and sanitation** services in participating ULBs." Chennai's flood-forecasting flagship is off-mission paper: carried in procurement annexes, absent from results.

## 2. The contracts: three years of slippage, then vanishing
From the Jun-2026 procurement plan (contract annex, verbatim statuses):
- **IN-TNUIFSL-350974-GO-RFB** (RTDAS + flood control rooms at SEOC/WRD Chepauk/4 collectorates, US$6,060,000): status **"Pending Implementation", disbursed $0.00**, bid opening sequence recorded as 2023-03-20 → 2023-11-15 → **2024-09-10** — i.e. eighteen months of advertised re-tendering, still unexecuted three years after first listing.
- **IN-TNUIFSL-351017-CS-CDS** (handholding-phase supervision, US$83,000): disbursed **$0.00** even in the Jun-2026 plan, revised completion 2025-10-15.
- **NEW related line**: **IN-TNUIFSL-375180-CS-CDS** (proof-checking of engineering designs/DPR for integrated storm-water drains, US$60,000): "Signed" → "Under Implementation", completions 2023-10-20 → 2024-04-17.
- **The Aug-2026 plan (base 2025-06-20, STEP-updated) drops all four** RTFF/SWD contract lines while still naming TNUIFSL as implementing agency. Removal while "Pending Implementation" at $0.00 disbursed reads as **descope from Bank-financed procurement** — cancelled, absorbed, or re-funded elsewhere. Nothing public says which. This is the sharpest single question the Bank's own paper now raises.

The $450M in the newest plan = $300M IBRD + $150M GoTN counterpart; TA component $30M ($21M IBRD + $9M GoTN). No additional financing — no change to §5.3 figures.

## 4. What the Bank's results framework pays for (and doesn't)
DLI architecture per PAD: DLI 3 (CCAPs incl. urban-drainage plans), DLI 6 (WSS subproject climate-resilience guidelines), DLI 7 (WSS operational efficiency) — **no indicator anywhere touches forecast accuracy, station uptime, alert delivery, or the RTFF**. Our §5.4(2) inference ("the verification was available for free as a disbursement condition — and wasn't taken") is now confirmed against the program document itself.
