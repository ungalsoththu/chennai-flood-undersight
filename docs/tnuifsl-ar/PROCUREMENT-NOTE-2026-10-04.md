## 2. WB PPSD (Sep 2023): CRTFF is TNCRUDP's #1 priority, and one contract jumped ships
From the Project Procurement Strategy for Development (`PPSD_2023-09.txt`, archived):
- Priority table: **"Setting up of RTDAS and Control Rooms for Chennai Real Time Flood Forecasting Project (CRTFF)" — Goods, US$6.06M Bank-financed — ranked priority 1** of the whole TNCRUDP TA program; handholding supervision consultancy ($0.10M) priority 2. [A]
- Narrative: *"Under TA for TNCRUDP, the Bank is to consider financing an activity procured in the TNSUDP… TNUIFSL notified the contract award on 13 Mar 2023 before the closure of the TNSUDP (31 Mar 2023). This contract management… will be managed under TNCRUDP STEP Portal."* The Bank reviewed through award and gave No Objection. **The PPSD does not name the awarded contract in the excerpt; identity is RTI-able to TNUIFSL. [A for the quote, I on the mapping]**
- Cross-check with the Dec-2023 PP: RTDAS package IN-TNUIFSL-350974-GO-RFB shows bid-opening sequence 2023-03-20 → 2023-11-15 → 2024-09-10 — i.e. a fresh Goods tender opening seven days *after* the 13-Mar award notification of "an activity procured in the TNSUDP". Two different papers, two different contracts, same weeks. [B — reconciliation inference]

## 4. What this means for "who paid the vendor"
Documented pipes that could have paid SECON-JBA and sub-vendors:
1. **PDGF (GoTN corpus, seeded by WB TNUDP/TNSUDP grants)** — the pipe the government names. No assignment-wise utilisation is public: TNUIFSL ARs are silent, PDGF page gives charter only.
2. **TNSUDP (P150395) TA** — the ICR books "RTFFS for Chennai" as a TNSUDP output (INR 100 crore); at least one contract award (13 Mar 2023) was made under TNSUDP and carried into TNCRUDP.
3. **TNCRUDP (P179189) TA** — CRTFF hardware is its priority-1 line, $0 disbursed through Jun 2026, dropped Aug 2026.
No single public document reconciles these three ledgers into one expenditure statement. That reconciliation is exactly what RTI to TNUIFSL (assignment-wise expenditure + utilisation certificates) and the GoTN sanction G.O. must produce.
