2.   OBJECTIVES      OF   THE    PUBLIC     WORKS
     DEPARTMENT

       Since 1858, the Tamil Nadu Public Works
Department      has   served     as   the   State
Government’s premier engineering organisation,
coordinating    and   executing     major  public
infrastructure   works,   including   Government
buildings, roads, bridges, irrigation facilities,
water resources development, drinking water
supply and power projects.

       Over    the    years,   population    growth,
urbanisation and technological advancements
have necessitated the expansion of public
infrastructure. Consequently, various functions of
the Public Works Department have been
progressively      reorganised     into    dedicated
Departments, Boards, and Corporations to
enhance      domain     expertise,     administrative
efficiency and effective service delivery.

      The Public Works Department has a
distinguished legacy of creating several iconic and
                          2


                          2
historically significant landmarks, including the
Public Works Department Building at Chennai, the
Madras High Court Building, the Connemara Public
Library, the Senate House of the University of
Madras and the Presidency College buildings. At
present, the Public Works Department is
efficiently undertaking the planning, design,
construction and maintenance of buildings to
meet the diverse requirements of various
departments of the State Government.

      The Public Works Department undertakes
the construction of Court Buildings, District
Collectorate,  Government     Medical    Colleges,
Hospitals and allied Medical Buildings, Veterinary
Hospitals, Government School and College
Buildings, Student Hostels, Government Industrial
Training Institutes, Government Homes, Taluk
Offices, Regional Transport Offices, Memorials,
Monuments and buildings for various other
Government Departments.

      The    Public    Works     Department      also
undertakes arrangements for official functions
during the visits of important dignitaries, including

stage erection, barricading and the provision of
public address systems and lighting facilities.

3. SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

     The   targets    under    the   Sustainable
Development Goals pertaining to the Public Works
Department are targets 9.1 and 11.4.

       Target 9.1 – Develop           Sustainable,
Resilient and Inclusive Infrastructures:
Develop quality, reliable, sustainable and resilient
infrastructure, including regional and transborder
infrastructure, to support economic development
and human well-being, with a focus on affordable
and equitable access for all.

     •   The Public Works Department actively
         promotes sustainable development by
         integrating the Sustainable Development
         Goals into all of its projects.
     •   The Public Works Department constructs
         buildings that ensure resource efficiency,
         incorporate climate change mitigation
         measures and enhance disaster resilience
         in alignment with the Sendai Framework
         for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030.

                         4


                         4
     • The Public Works Department promotes
       the use of M-sand as an alternative to
       river sand in Government and private
       buildings    to   reduce    environmental
       degradation.
     • The Public Works Department grants
       Product Quality Approval certificates to
       M-sand manufacturers to ensure the
       quality of M-sand.
     • The Public Works Department promotes
       the use of fly ash bricks to facilitate the
       effective recycling of waste materials.

      Target 11.4 – Protect        the    World’s
Cultural        and       Natural       Heritage:
To strengthen efforts to protect and safeguard the
world’s cultural and natural heritage.

     •   To preserve and conserve heritage
         structures, the Public Works Department
         has established a separate Heritage
         Building Circle.
     •   The Public Works Department gives
         special emphasis on the use of traditional
         construction    techniques     for     the

         conservation, renovation and restoration
         of heritage structures.

      By integrating the Sustainable Development
Goals into its core mission, the Public Works
Department is contributing significantly to a
sustainable future.

4.   WINGS   OF         THE     PUBLIC       WORKS
     DEPARTMENT

4.1. Construction and Maintenance Wing

      The Construction and Maintenance Wing of
the Public Works Department is the primary
execution wing, comprising 4 Regions, Chennai,
Madurai, Tiruchirappalli and Coimbatore, 13 Circles
and 43 Divisions.

        The districts under the control of the
Chennai     Region    are     Chennai,     Tiruvallur,
Chengalpattu,       Kancheepuram,         Villupuram,
Tiruvannamalai, Kallakurichi, Tirupattur, Vellore
and Ranipet. The districts under the control of
Coimbatore Region are Coimbatore, Tiruppur, The
Nilgiris, Salem, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, Erode,
Karur and Namakkal. The districts under the
control of Tiruchirappalli Region are Tiruchirappalli,
                          6


                          6
Ariyalur,       Perambalur,        Nagapattinam,
Mayiladuthurai, Tiruvarur, Thanjavur, Pudukkottai
and Cuddalore. The districts under the control of
Madurai Region are Madurai, Theni, Dindigul,
Sivaganga,    Ramanathapuram,      Virudhunagar,
Tirunelveli,    Tenkasi,     Thoothukudi     and
Kanniyakumari.

4.2. Projects Wing

      The Projects wing functions under the
control of the Chief Engineer, Chennai, and
comprises     Divisions  located at   Chennai,
Coimbatore, Madurai, Thanjavur, Tiruchirappalli,
Tirunelveli and Vellore.

      Building construction and maintenance
works are being carried out for all Government
Engineering Colleges, Government Polytechnic
Colleges, Government Arts and Science Colleges,
Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University
including Government Colleges of Education,
special institutions and constituent colleges of
various universities, including Anna University.

4.3. ARCHITECT WING

     The Architect wing of the Public Works
Department functions under the control of the
Chief Architect. The Office of the Chief Architect
undertakes the planning, design and preparation
of architectural details for Government buildings
and related built environments across the State.

      In the context of global challenges such as
climate     change,   rising   temperatures     and
increasing demands on natural resources, the
Office of the Chief Architect continues to promote
sustainable construction practices. Emphasis is laid
on integrating environmental sensitivity, universal
accessibility,   user     comfort    and    modern
technological advancements into all Government
building projects.

     The Architect wing continues to keep
adopting the latest software related to 3D design
to keep pace with technology changes and to stay
in the forefront for providing better services to all
Government departments.

4.4. PLANNING AND DESIGN CIRCLE

      The primary role of the Planning and Designs
Circle is to prepare structural designs and detailed
drawings based on the architectural plans
prepared by the Chief Architect, Public Works
Department, in accordance with the requirements
of the user departments and to scrutinise the
detailed estimates prepared by the field engineers
for the construction of Government buildings,
ensuring compliance with the standards prescribed
by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

      This wing develops structural designs for all
Government      buildings,    such    as     district
collectorates, medical colleges, hospitals, primary
health centres, schools, memorials, courts, law
colleges, Commercial Taxes and Registration
Department offices, hostels for Backward Class
and Most Backward Class students, special
schools, rehabilitation homes, circuit houses,
museums, libraries, observation homes, guest
houses, monuments and Government staff
quarters.

     Upon receipt of requests from the licensing
authorities for cinema theatres, namely the
                          9


                         9
Commissioner of Police for Chennai and the
District Collectors for the other districts, the
Planning and Design Circle conducts site inspection
and forwards the Structural Soundness Certificate,
along with the plan recommendations, to the
respective licensing authority.

      Structural Stability Certificate to the Public
Buildings which are more than 50 years old are
issued by the Planning and Design Circle after site
inspection.

4.5. TAMIZHAGA      ARASU               BUILDING
      RESEARCH STATION

       Through the Public Works Department
Assessment Committee, the Tamilzhaga Arasu
Building Research Station is engaged in assessing
the quality of various civil and electrical
construction materials, including M-sand, paint,
steel, cement, construction chemicals, doors,
windows, PVC pipes, tiles, electrical wires,
switches, lighting fixtures, conduit pipes and other
special or innovative construction materials
introduced in the market.

technical experts from various Government
institutions and departments, such as the Indian
Institute of Technology (IIT), the Bureau of Indian

      The Common Schedule of Rates is prepared
every year after holding discussions with various
departments of the Government and the
contractor’s associations and is issued with the
concurrence of the Finance Department.

scrutiny of estimates and preparation of structural

various     Government     departments,      quasi-
Government organisations and other entities.


      Furthermore, laboratory testing for quality
assessment of construction materials, such as
cement, steel and concrete, is carried out at the
laboratory established at Taramani, Chennai.

4.6. CONSERVATION,            RENOVATION       AND
     RESTORATION OF HERITAGE BUILDINGS

       The Heritage Wing is entrusted with the
conservation, renovation and restoration of
heritage buildings. This wing functions with
division offices at Chennai and Madurai.

       The heritage buildings are restored and
renovated     in   accordance    with    established
conservation principles and national best practices.
Special emphasis is being given to retaining
original architectural features by using traditional
construction techniques, such as lime mortar
plastering, Madras terrace roofing, traditional brick
masonry, heritage timber elements, art plaster
work and decorative finishes.

4.7. QUALITY CONTROL WING

       The Public Works Department undertakes
the construction of numerous public buildings,
ensuring the highest standards of quality. The
Quality Control Wing functions with regional
divisions located at Chennai, Tiruchirappalli,
Madurai and Coimbatore, to inspect and improve
the quality of buildings.
                         12


                         12
       Quality   Control  Engineers   carry    out
inspections of ongoing works executed by the
Public Works Department to ensure compliance
with quality control measures, codal provisions of
the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and
structural design requirements.

        Further, tests on hardened concrete and
other construction materials are conducted
through Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) methods,
field tests and laboratory tests, and the results of
laboratory tests are communicated to the field
officials concerned. Observations made during site
inspections are communicated by the Quality
Control Engineers to the field officials concerned
for necessary rectification and compliance, and the
action taken reports submitted by the field officials
are reviewed by the Quality Control Wing. After
review, Quality Control Certificates are issued by
the Quality Control Wing at various stages of
execution of the Public Works Department
projects.

      Through these activities, the Quality Control
Wing plays a vital role in enhancing and sustaining
the quality of construction works executed by the

                         13


                         13
Department, thereby improving the durability and
service life of public buildings.

4.8. PUBLIC      WORKS        STAFF     TRAINING
     INSTITUTE

       The Public Works Staff Training Institute in

4.9. ELECTRICAL WING

       The Electrical Wing functions with 13
Electrical Divisions to execute electrical works for
the buildings and structures constructed by the
Public Works Department.

     This wing also undertakes works to adopt
modern technologies in electrical systems.

       Since 1968, this Wing undertakes all
internal and external electrification works in
buildings,    including specialised    electrical

                         14


                        14
installations, such as air-conditioning facilities,
lifts, generators, transformers, public address
systems, lighting facilities, intercom systems,
video     conferencing   systems,    Closed-Circuit
Television (CCTV) systems, fire alarm systems and
special lighting arrangements for monuments and
memorials.

      Electrical installation and maintenance
works in Government buildings are carried out
without any interruption.

       The Electrical Wing of the Public Works
Department executes all electrical works in
accordance with the provisions of the Electricity
Act, 2003    and      the    Central     Electricity
Authority (Measures Relating to Safety and Electric
Supply) Regulations, 2023.


4.10. DIRECTORATE OF BOILERS

       The Office of the Director of Boilers
functions under the Public Works Department. The
Director of Boilers ensures the safe design of the
boilers and boiler components as per the
provisions of the Boilers Act, 2025 and the Indian

                         15


                        15
Boiler Regulations. The Director of Boilers provides
the approval for the manufacturing units of boilers
and boiler components, the Non-Destructive
Testing Firms and the boilers repairers and
erectors.


       It carries out tasks such as inspecting
boilers, certifying boilers and boiler components
during manufacturing and registering new boilers.


        The Director of Boilers serves as the
Chairman of the Board of Examiners for issue of
certificates to Boiler Operation Engineers and
Boiler Attendants according to the Boiler Operation
Engineers Rules, 2025 and the Boiler Attendants
Rules, 2025, respectively.


        In the year 2025-2026, 580 boilers have
been inspected during manufacture and certified,
and 5,836 boilers have been inspected and
certified for operation in industries. A revenue of
Rs.8.56 crore has been generated from the above
inspections.
