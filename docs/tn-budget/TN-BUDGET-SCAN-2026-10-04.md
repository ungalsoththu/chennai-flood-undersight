# TN budget scan: the ₹107.2-crore system that never appears in the budget — 2026-10-04

Question: does the Tamil Nadu budget (demands, policy notes, speeches) ever show a budgetary allocation for the Real-Time Flood Forecasting & Spatial Decision Support System?

## Findings

### 1. The project's budgetary home is Demand No. 34 (MAWS) — and TNUIFSL is its pipe
The MAWS policy note 2025-26 lists TNUIFSL as parastatal #6 and profiles it (§7): manager of TNUDF (corpus ₹199.60 crore) and **four external credit lines** (§7.2): TNUFIP (ADB, 3 phases), MID-TN (KfW), SMIF-TN-III (KfW), **TNCRUDP (World Bank)** — "Funds totaling **₹17,297.14 crore**... with external assistance of **₹10,691.39 crore**" (Table 7.1). TNCRUDP appears ~12× across the note with scheme-level works (e.g. ₹59.92 crore packages).

### 2. PDGF — the fund that issued the ₹107.2-crore sanction — is never named
- **Zero "PDGF" occurrences** in MAWS policy notes FY2024-25 and FY2025-26 (the sanction's own years); TNUIFSL's grant funds are described only as "various other Government Grant Funds".
- Zero in Finance policy notes (FY2021-22, FY2022-23) and the Revised Budget 2026-27 speech.
- The only public accounting of PDGF remains TNUIFSL's own webpage — grants-in-aid ₹35.23 crore (2023-24), **nil in 2024-25** (first nil year since the 2015 inception), nil in 2025-26 listed.

### 3. RTFF & SDSS is invisible in every budget document scanned
- MAWS §2.3.5 "Flood Mitigation Works" (both years): only stormwater drains, each with G.O. citations (G.O.214/RD&PR 17.05.2022 ₹82.15 cr; G.O.305/RD 21.06.2023 ₹145.24 cr; ADB ₹82.98 cr; SDMF ₹12.00 cr) — **no forecasting system, no ₹107.2 crore, no CRTFF**.
- Revised Budget 2026-27 speech: exactly one urban-flooding para (¶109, blue-green infrastructure around Chennai water bodies); no RTFF/PDGF/TNCRUDP announcements.
- CMDA/HUD/Finance policy notes: no hits.

### 4. The contrast: TNSUDP tech money IS publicized — for other projects
GCC's **GIS & Drone Survey Phase-I under "the World Bank assisted TNSUDP, at a cost of ₹6.44 crore"** appears in the MAWS policy note with outcome claims (78,212 property-tax reassessments → ₹154 crore additional annual demand). The Bank-project label was used loudly for that; the forecasting flagship never got the same budget-paper treatment.

### 5. The drains side is loudly budgeted; the forecast side never is
GCC's own FY2026-27 budget: **₹1,341 crore climate budget** for 240+ km of stormwater drains (verified in `200-QUESTIONS.md` Q194). A ₹107.2-crore system that never appears in state or city budget narratives sits inside that asymmetry.

## What this means for the audit

The only state-side paper for RTFF & SDSS is the 2022 G.O. sanction flowing through PDGF — a fund the budget never itemizes. That is exactly how money escapes demand-level scrutiny: a non-lapsable grant fund + a company (TNUIFSL) outside the demand structure. The heads of account that would close this (Demand 34 detailed demand: PDGF transfer head, TNCRUDP state-share head) are the RTI target.
