## What the budget says (verified)

**Revenue & Disaster Management (Demand 16) — Policy Note FY2026-27**
(`revenue_e_pn_2026_27.pdf`, 3.1 MB, fetched 2026-10-04; full text `revenue_e_pn_2026_27.txt`):

1. **§3.3.6 Integrated Flood Management Centre (IFMC)** — constituted under **TNDRRA** by
   **G.O. (Ms) No. 749, Revenue and Disaster Management Department, dated 23.10.2025**,
   "to operationalize and further strengthen the flood forecasting and Hydro-modelling capabilities
   developed under the Chennai Real-Time Flood Forecasting and Spatial Decision Support System
   (CRTFF & SDSS), including the functions envisaged under the Hydro-Modelling Control Room (HMCR)."
   → IFMC = successor body; constituted 3 months before the Oct-2025 "soft launch" we audited.
   → Budget line for IFMC operations: **not shown** in the note.

2. **§3.4.1.2.4 Chennai Real Time Flood Forecasting Project (CRTFF)** — "the Government of Tamil Nadu,
   with World Bank assistance, is implementing ... (CRTFF & SDSS) at an outlay of **Rs.122.76 crore**."
   → **Third cost figure.** ₹71 cr (Nov 2022, sensor-expansion phase) → ₹107.2 cr (Oct 2025 launch
   coverage) → **₹122.76 cr** (FY2026-27 policy note). +15% over the launch figure, no public
   sanction/revision trail. Head-of-account lines not in the policy note.

3. **§3.4.1.2.3 state IoT network** — 64 ARGs + 100 AWSs "at a sanctioned cost of Rs.32.48 crore";
   "Upon the go-live of the Chennai Real-Time Flood Forecasting Project," +78 ARGs, +14 AWSs,
   +145 AWLRs join the network. IITM-Pune × TNSDMA joint site survey April–June 2026.
   → Confirms the expansion phase is budgeted inside Revenue/DMA, not MAWS.

4. **PDGF: zero mentions** in the Revenue note. The grant layer remains publicly unconnected to
   the project in any GoTN budget document we hold.

**MAWS (Demand 34) FY2024-25 & FY2025-26** (Wayback captures): zero RTFF/CRTFF/PDGF/₹107.2 mentions;
TNSUDP cited once (₹6.44-cr GCC GIS/drone survey); flood chapter = drains only. The department that
houses TNUIFSL never narrates the project the fund manages.

**Finance (Demand 12) FY2021-22/FY2022-23**: PDGF appears only as a statutory-body line in Part-II
appropriations; no project breakdown.
